Cite COAL

If you use COAL in published work, please cite the version you used. Use coal --version for a specific model reference.

References

Newton C, Murati K (2026). COAL: codon alignments from protein alignments, version 0.6.0. https://github.com/plumat-coal/COAL

COAL follows PAL2NAL's method, so please cite PAL2NAL too:

Suyama M, Torrents D, Bork P (2006). PAL2NAL: robust conversion of protein sequence alignments into the corresponding codon alignments. Nucleic Acids Research 34:W609–W612.

CITATION.cff

What to cite for what you ran

COAL's Ka/Ks (dN/dS) numbers come from published estimators, in code derived from KaKs_Calculator 2.0, PAML, libsequence and seqinr, and coal pipeline runs DIAMOND and an aligner. Please cite what your numbers come from, with COAL. coal cite prints this table; coal cite out/report.json prints the references of one pipeline run.
